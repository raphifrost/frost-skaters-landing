📚 Skating tips & guide

For new members

Your first steps

Apply using your first name, request a community card, read the rink rules and treat others respectfully. Questions? Ask the team.

Apply for a member card
Skates

Taking care of your skates

Dry blades after skating, use blade guards off the ice and let your skates dry completely. Have a professional check if your blades need sharpening.

Safety

Skate carefully

Keep a safe distance, watch out for beginners, use permitted areas and ask for help if unsure. Wear suitable protective equipment.

For trips

Checklist

  • Skates or rentals
  • Suitable clothing and gloves
  • Travel ticket and entry fee
  • Water for the journey
  • Parent/guardian permission for minors

🛡️ Our rules

Respect everyone. Follow the rink rules. Skate carefully. Always ask before taking photos.

In case of an accident, tell rink staff and a responsible adult immediately. In an emergency, call 112.
